8-Bits privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This is a translation. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

This policy explains what information the 8-Bits app ("the app") uses, what it uses it for and what choices you have. The app is a collection of 8-bit style video games. It requires no sign-up, has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

1. Controller

Briam Steven Orozco Buitrago (Gem), Colombia. Contact for any privacy matter: briamsteven8@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress is stored only on your phone (the app's local storage): coins, records, unlocked games, outfits, skins and companions, themes, language and settings (sound, vibration, aim assist). This information is not sent to our servers. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is lost; purchases made with real money can be recovered with "Restore purchases".

3. Information processed by third parties

ServicePurposeData
Google AdMob (Google LLC)Show ads between games and optional rewarded ads. Device advertising ID, IP address, technical device data and ad interaction data. If you do not give consent, ads will be non-personalized.
Google Play / Apple App StoreProcess in-app purchases. The stores handle the payment. The app only receives the purchase confirmation; it never sees your card details.
8-Bits room serverConnect two phones in "2 players · 2 phones" mode. IP address (needed for the connection), the room code and the game messages (buttons pressed and game images). They are not stored: they are relayed in real time and discarded when the room ends.
Direct connection between players (WebRTC, with Google STUN servers)Let both phones play without going through the server. Both devices' IP addresses are exchanged to set up the direct connection.

Third-party policies: Google, Apple.

4. Consent and advertising

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before using data for personalized advertising. You can change your choice later from the app's settings ("Ad privacy").
  • On iPhone and iPad, the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, Apple's advertising identifier is not used and ads will not be personalized.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

6. Retention

We do not keep personal data on our servers. Data processed by Google or Apple is kept according to their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country (for example the GDPR in Europe or the LGPD in Brazil), you can request access, correction, deletion or objection to the processing of your data. Since the app does not store your data on our servers, you can delete all your progress from the app with "RESET PROGRESS" or by uninstalling it. For any request, write to briamsteven8@gmail.com.

8. Security

Online mode connections are encrypted (WSS and WebRTC). Even so, no system is 100% secure.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is significant, we will announce it inside the app.
