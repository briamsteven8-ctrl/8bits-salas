8-BITS

A game by Gem

Dozens of 8-bit style arcade games in one app: classics, adventures, sports and a two-player mode. Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

What's inside

  • Retro arcade, action, puzzle, adventure and sports games.
  • Two-player mode on the same PC or with two phones in a room.
  • Outfits and companions for your heroes, and skins for your favorite games.
  • No sign-up, and you can play offline (the two-phone mode needs internet).

NEW Sports to challenge a friend

Each one with its own mechanic. Play against the computer or against another person.

BasketballCharge your shot and release it in the green zone. Steal, block and hit threes.
Beach VolleyJump and spike at sunset. Up to 3 touches per side.
Air HockeyThe puck takes the power of your hit and bounces off the rails.
BoxingGuard, hook and counterpunch, round by round.
BaseballThrow curves and fastballs, or swing at just the right moment.
SumoCharge your rush and push your rival out of the ring.
Mini GolfHoles with a windmill, water, sand and ramps.
HurdlesAlternate ← → to run and jump the hurdles.
